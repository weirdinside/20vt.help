a very basic C4 REAR coilover DIY
(based on writeups from EDIGREG and pilihp2)

shocks (~$270)
- get a shock of your choice (I used bilstein B8, but you can also use a compatible koni)
- you will want to remove the perch on the bilstein. IF YOU USE A KONI YOU WILL NEED THESE:(https://www.amazon.com/.../ref=ppx_yo_dt_b_search_asin...) - these add $64 to the total. You can find cheaper versions on amazon as well, just search for a 2" ID clamping collar.
*Stewart Coston also recommends using the above even if you have a bilstein shock. Just for the added insurance.

shock body ($108):
- 2x bottom spring perch (https://www.a1racing.com/.../2_Spring_Coil_Over_Adjusting...)
- 2x top spring perch (https://www.a1racing.com/.../2_Spring_NonSlotted_Coil...)
- 2x coilover sleeve (FOR BILSTEINS)(https://www.a1racing.com/A1_Racing_Products_12451_5_Coil...) - EDIGREG says 12452-H, but 12451 is the right one as mentioned by QW user wheeljack (https://forums.quattroworld.com/s4s6/msgs/312622.phtml)
(FOR KONIS - you need the sleeve collar too, and this sleeve instead https://www.a1racing.com/A1_Racing_Products_12450_5_Coil...)


(I haven't tried these two below, but assume they also work)

OR ($104)

- 2x coilover sleeve kit [https://carolinaracingsupply.com/.../BILSTEIN-COIL-OVER...]

OR ($112)

- 2x coilover sleeve kit [https://www.speedwaymotors.com/Coilover-Kits-2-Inch-Body...]

I used a 7" spring with a helper spring, but if you want a more aggressive setup you can opt to go for just the spring alone. If you want to run a coilover bag (as pilihp2 recommends), you should go with a barrel spring instead of the regular ones I've listed here.

- 2x helper spring (https://pitstopusa.com/i-5077505-hypercoils-4-take-up...)
- 2x spring spacer (https://pitstopusa.com/i-5069781-eibach-2-1-2-i-d-spring...) [this goes inbetween the helper and the main spring]
- 2x 7" 500lb eibach spring (https://pitstopusa.com/i-5061836-eibach-7-coil-over...) [you can choose to replace this with a different spring rate spring if you want, but these feel great on my DD UrS6 avant]

(this totals to ~$290, you might be able to find better prices elsewhere)

Instead of doing all that, you can opt to just get the 7" spring (last item in the last part of the writeup) or get these barrel springs pilihp2 recommends:

- 2x barrel springs (https://eibach.com/product/0700.2530.0500) [once again, choose rate based on your preference]

(this totals to $170 instead. much more affordable, though I haven't tested just using one spring. it's probably fine)

While not necessary at all, I also recommend putting an O ring between the shock body and the sleeve if there is excessive play. You don't want it to seize on there, but you also don't want any play between the two.

Instructions can be found here, thanks pilihp2: https://theprojectpad.com/viewtopic.php?f=28&t=3068
- modify as per your preference
- As both EDIGREG and pilihp2 recommend, cut the top mount as shown in the project pad photos. It'll be a bit of a wrestling match getting it back into the car as there's less rubber moving around, but it is worth it to get the extra threads.

DO NOT forget the little washer that goes on the shock threads. if you assemble without it, the strut will hit the body of the car, you'll hear a clunking and be wondering where it's coming from.

cost:
Total with sleeve perch + all fixins: $732
Total with sleeve perch and no helper spring: $612

shock/spring combo:
Total with bilstein + H&R: ~$540 (these include front springs too, but you can't really buy rear springs separate from the fronts unfortunately)

cost may vary as well based on where you're located and where you can find them. these are NOT the best prices for these items, just options that I came across while building my setup. on average, it's about $200 more than a traditional shock/spring setup, but I think the money is well worth it for the adjustability and customizability.
